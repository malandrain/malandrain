## About me:
PhD student in Glaciology at Uppsala University modelling the future of Riiser-Larsen Ice shelf using UFEMISM model and collecting new GPR and ApRES data across 2 campaigns to Antarctica. I also have projects ongoing in the Arctic, mostly Svalbard where I am interested in developing observatories in glacier-fjord systems.

I also have been interrested in Glacial Hazard in the Alps and used the finite element model Elmer/Ice to assess the risk of a potential collapse.

Other than that, I work with drone photogrammetry, feature tracking on timelapse and satellite data and like to contribute in developping more open science and low tech approaches when studying the cryosphere.
